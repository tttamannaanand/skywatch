    Exoplanet hunter · TESS light curves

    Finding planets around other stars

    When a planet crosses in front of its star, the star dims a tiny bit, the same way every orbit. Skywatch downloaded real brightness measurements from NASA's TESS telescope and searched them for those dips, then checked its answers against NASA's Exoplanet Archive.
